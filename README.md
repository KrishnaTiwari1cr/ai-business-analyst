# 🤖 AI Business Analyst

> An LLM-powered business analytics agent that allows users to ask natural-language questions about business data and automatically generates SQL, validates it, executes it, performs analysis, creates visualizations, and produces business-friendly insights.

---

## 📌 Overview

AI Business Analyst is an intelligent analytics application built using **LangGraph, Python, PostgreSQL, Gemini, and Groq**.

Instead of manually writing SQL queries, a business user can simply ask questions such as:

- Which product category generated the most revenue?
- What is the total revenue?
- What are the top 5 products by revenue?
- How did revenue change over the last 6 months?
- Why did revenue drop?
- Which categories contributed most to the decline?

The system converts the natural-language question into a complete analytical workflow.
